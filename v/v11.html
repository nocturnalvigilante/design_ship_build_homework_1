A model that knows a tumour is there, and cannot say where.

Text-conditioned 3D medical models detect small lesions and then fail to localize them. This is what that failure is, and what fixes it.

One

The failure gets worse as the lesion gets smaller, and never recovers.

Sorted by lesion volume into terciles, localization declines monotonically in every region. For enhancing tumour, the clinically urgent case, Dice falls by a factor of 14.9 from the largest third to the smallest.

Enhancing tumour, held-out n = 73. Whole tumour falls 4.9× and tumour core 9.3× over the same range.

Two

The lesions are findable. The conditioning is what fails.

The obvious objection is that small lesions are simply hard. So the same lesions went to a plain supervised U-Net, no language involved at all.

1.2×

degradation, supervised U-Net, same small lesions

14.9×

degradation, text-conditioned model, enhancing tumour

Three

And the language contributes nothing measurable.

Replacing PubMedBERT with random vectors changes almost nothing. Whatever the model is doing with the prompt, it is not reading it.

The query functions as an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English.

Four

The cheapest available fix beat every elaborate one.

Four interventions were tried. Three were reversed after a control caught the error.

  • Size-conditioned prompting. Helped small lesions, broke large ones.
  • Multi-scale ensembling. Gained on tumour core, gave it back on enhancing.
  • Scale-matched retraining. Overfit the tercile it was tuned on.
  • Centre-weighted window merging. +0.145 Dice, no retraining.

The volume is processed in overlapping sliding windows and merged by flat averaging, which smears a small lesion across everything near a window edge. Weighting each window toward its own centre recovers small enhancing tumour in 7 of 21 patients where the model previously found none, and changes no weights at all.

A shared confound is not a cancelled confound.

Every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it. That is the part of this worth carrying to the next dataset.
