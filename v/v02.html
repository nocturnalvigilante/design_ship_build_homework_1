01 Reported failure

The model detects that a lesion is present, then fails to localize it once the lesion is small.

02 Measurements on file

RegionLargeMedium SmallRatio
Whole tumour0.2810.1370.0574.9×

Supervised U-Net (control)———1.2×

The control degrades 1.2× on the same lesions. They are learnable. The text conditioning is what fails.

03 Lines of enquiry

× Is the failure real? Yes. Monotonic in lesion size, 171 paired Wilcoxon tests with Benjamini‑Hochberg correction, replicated across three splits.
× Is it linguistic? No. Replacing PubMedBERT with random vectors changes almost nothing. The query is an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English.
× Can it be fixed? Yes, and cheaply. Reweighting each window toward its centre gives +0.145 Dice with no retraining, recovering small enhancing tumour in 7 of 21 patients where it previously found none.

04 Note for the record

Three experiments were reversed after controls caught errors. A shared confound is not a cancelled confound: every stage of the pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.
