Text-conditioned 3D medical models do not localize small lesions. They detect them and then guess.

Four claims. Each one is a measurement, not an opinion. The numbers below are held-out, corrected, and replicated across three independent splits.

  1. The failure is monotonic in lesion size.

    Dice falls from large to small in every region, and never recovers. For enhancing tumour it falls by a factor of fifteen.

    RegionLargeMediumSmallRatio
    Whole tumour0.2810.1370.0574.9×
    Tumour core0.1760.0590.0199.3×
    Enhancing tumour0.1490.0370.01014.9×

  2. The lesions are learnable. The conditioning is not.

    A plain supervised U-Net, given the same small lesions, degrades by a factor of 1.2. The data is not the problem.

    1.2× VS 14.9×

  3. The language contributes nothing.

    Replace PubMedBERT with random vectors and almost nothing changes. The query functions as an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English.

    Δ DICE ≈ 0.000

  4. The cheapest fix won.

    Size-conditioned prompting, multi-scale ensembling and scale-matched retraining each traded one failure for another. Reweighting every sliding window toward its own centre beat all of them and changed no weights at all. Small enhancing tumour went from 0 to 7 of 21 patients.

    +0.145 DICE · NO RETRAINING

A shared confound is not a cancelled confound.

Three results in this project were reversed after a control caught the error. Every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.
