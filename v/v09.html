The fix changed no weights

Same model. Same scan. One line of merging code.

Text-conditioned 3D models smear small lesions across every sliding window that touches them. Weight each window toward its own centre instead and the prediction snaps back onto the tumour. No retraining, no fine-tuning, no extra data.

Why the cheap fix works

Because the failure was never about language.

Localization degrades monotonically with lesion size, in every region, with no recovery. Enhancing tumour falls 14.9× from the large tercile to the small one.

A plain supervised U-Net on the same small lesions degrades 1.2×. They are learnable. It is the conditioning that fails.

Replace PubMedBERT with random vectors and almost nothing changes. The query functions as an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English, so no amount of better text was ever going to help.

What was left was the plumbing. The volume is processed in overlapping windows and merged by flat averaging, which spreads a small lesion sitting near a window edge across everything around it.
