Differential diagnosis

  • ✗Small lesions are simply unfindable.

    Excluded. A supervised U-Net on the same lesions degrades 1.2× against 14.9×. They are learnable.

  • ✗The text encoder is too weak.

    Excluded. Replacing PubMedBERT with random vectors changes almost nothing. The query is an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English.

  • ✓The windows are merged badly.

    Confirmed. The volume is traversed in overlapping sliding windows and averaged flat, which smears a small lesion across everything near a window edge.

Notes on the record

Three earlier interventions were commenced and withdrawn after a control caught the error: size-conditioned prompting, multi-scale ensembling and scale-matched retraining. A shared confound is not a cancelled confound. Every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.

171 paired Wilcoxon tests, Benjamini‑Hochberg corrected. 9 of 9 comparisons replicated across three independent splits.
