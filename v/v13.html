Revision history — four hypotheses

RevChangeStatus A Small lesions are unfindable. Supervised U-Net, same lesions, no language anywhere in the pipeline. Superseded · 1.2× B The text encoder is too weak. PubMedBERT replaced with random vectors. The query is an opaque class identifier spelled in English. Superseded · Δ≈0.000 C Tell the model the lesion is small. Size-conditioned prompting, multi-scale ensembling, scale-matched retraining. Superseded · 3 of 3 D The sliding windows are merged badly. Flat averaging smears a small lesion across everything near a window edge. Weight each window toward its own centre. Issued · +0.145

General note

A shared confound is not a cancelled confound

The fault was not in the model and not in the language. It sat in the inference plumbing, where nobody was looking, and the repair modifies no weights at all. Small enhancing tumour recovered in 7 of 21 patients where the model previously found none.

Every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.
