Localization failure — text-conditioned 3D medical models

It knows the tumour is there. It cannot say where.

The model detects a lesion correctly, then returns a region many times too large and centred somewhere else. The smaller the lesion, the wider the miss — and the small ones are the clinically urgent ones.
