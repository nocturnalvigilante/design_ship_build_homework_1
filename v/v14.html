02  Three questions

Real, not linguistic, and cheap to fix.

Is the failure real?

Yes, and it reproduces.

9 / 9

171 paired Wilcoxon tests, Benjamini‑Hochberg corrected, replicated across three independent splits.

Is it linguistic?

No. The prompt does nothing.

≈ 0

Swapping PubMedBERT for random vectors changes almost nothing. The query is an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English.

Can it be fixed?

Yes, without retraining.

+0.145

Weight each sliding window toward its own centre. Small enhancing tumour goes from 0 to 7 of 21 patients.

The simplest intervention beat every elaborate one. No retraining at all.

Size-conditioned prompting, multi-scale ensembling and scale-matched retraining each solved part of the problem and introduced a new failure. All three were reversed after a control caught the error.

The volume is traversed in overlapping sliding windows merged by flat average, which smears a small lesion across everything near a window edge. Weighting each window toward its own centre repairs it and touches no weights.
