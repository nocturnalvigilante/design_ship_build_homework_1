Small-lesion localization

It finds it.
It can’t place it.

Text-conditioned 3D medical models detect that a tumour is present, then return a region many times too large. The smaller the lesion, the wider the miss.

A shared confound is not a cancelled confound.

The fault was not in the model and not in the language. It sat in the inference plumbing, where nobody was looking. Every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.

1. Dice measured on held-out patients (n = 73) from BraTS2020, 369 patients, 128³ volumes. Terciles are formed by lesion volume within each region.

2. Detection remains unaffected at every lesion size. Aggregate scores computed over an unstratified test set do not surface this behaviour, which is how the failure went unremarked.

3. The supervised control is reported as a degradation ratio only; its absolute Dice is not directly comparable to the text-conditioned model.

4. Statistics: 171 paired Wilcoxon tests with Benjamini‑Hochberg correction. 9 of 9 comparisons replicated across three independent splits. Three earlier interventions were reversed after their controls were run.
