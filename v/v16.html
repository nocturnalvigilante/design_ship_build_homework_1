Text-conditioned 3D medical localization

Four lines left the junction. Three were closed.

These models detect that a tumour is present, then return a region many times too large and centred somewhere else. The map below is how the cause was found — every branch was ridden to its terminus before the through route was.

Each branch was measured against its own control before being closed. 171 paired Wilcoxon tests · BH corrected · 9 / 9 replicated

Line guide

What was found at each terminus.

Line 1 · Closed

The data

Perhaps small lesions are simply unfindable. A plain supervised U-Net was given the same lesions with no language anywhere in the pipeline.

Degrades 1.2×

Line 2 · Closed

The language

Perhaps the text encoder is too weak. PubMedBERT was replaced with random vectors. The query is an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English.

Change ≈ 0.000

Line 3 · Closed

Three remedies

Size-conditioned prompting, multi-scale ensembling and scale-matched retraining. Each traded one failure mode for another and was reversed once its control ran.

Reversed 3 of 3

Line 4 · Through route

Window merging

Flat averaging smears a small lesion across everything near a window edge. Weight each window toward its own centre. No weights modified.
