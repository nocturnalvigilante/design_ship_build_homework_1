LapataThe lost lesion

“It swore the tumour was there.
It would not say where.”

Text-conditioned 3D medical models detect that a lesion is present, then return a region many times too large and centred somewhere else. The smaller the lesion, the wider the miss.

Sixteen times the volume. Two centimetres from the truth. Dice 0.037.

The four accused

Suspect one

The Data

A plain supervised U-Net, given the same lesions and no language at all, barely falters. The lesions were always findable.

Acquitted · 1.2×

Suspect two

The Language

PubMedBERT torn out, random vectors put in its place. Nothing moved. The query is an opaque class id spelled in English.

Acquitted · Δ≈0

Suspect three

The Clever Remedies

Size-conditioned prompting, multi-scale ensembling, scale-matched retraining. All three reversed by their own controls.

Acquitted · 3 of 3

The culprit

The Plumbing

Flat window averaging smears a small lesion across all it passes. Weight each window toward its centre. No retraining.
