02  Three questions

Real, linguistic, fixable.

Is the failure real?

Yes.

9 / 9

Comparisons replicated across three independent splits. 171 paired Wilcoxon tests, Benjamini‑Hochberg corrected.

Is it linguistic?

No.

0

Swapping PubMedBERT for random vectors changes almost nothing. The query is an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English.

Can it be fixed?

Yes, cheaply.

+0.145

Reweight each window toward its centre. No retraining. Small enhancing tumour goes from 0 to 7 of 21 patients.

03  Finding

The simplest intervention beat every
elaborate one. No retraining at all.

Size-conditioned prompting, multi-scale ensembling and scale-matched retraining each solved part of the problem and introduced a new failure mode. Reweighting the window toward its centre solved more than all of them and touched no weights.

A shared confound is not a cancelled confound. Every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.
