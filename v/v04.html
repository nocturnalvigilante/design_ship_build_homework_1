Episode I

The Phantom Lesion

It is a period of confident benchmarks. Text-conditioned 3D models report strong aggregate scores across the BRATS archive, and the field has accepted them.

But the numbers conceal a weakness. Asked whether a tumour is present, the model answers correctly. Asked WHERE it is, it returns a region sixteen times too large and centred somewhere else entirely.

And the smaller the lesion, the wider it misses. Pursued across three independent splits, one analyst has traced the fault to an unguarded stage of the pipeline, where a two-line change may yet restore what was lost….

The exhaust port

It was never the model. It was the plumbing.

The fault sat at inference time, in a merge step nobody had thought to measure, and the repair costs no retraining and not one modified weight. Small enhancing tumour recovered in 7 of 21 subjects where the system previously found none.

Three more elaborate interventions were flown first and every one was reversed after a control caught it. A shared confound is not a cancelled confound: every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.

171 paired Wilcoxon tests · Benjamini‑Hochberg corrected · 9 of 9 replicated across three independent splits.
