Things I tried

01 Size-conditioned prompting. Tell the model the lesion is small. Helped small lesions, broke large ones.
02 Multi-scale ensembling. Averaged three resolutions. Gained on core, lost the gain on enhancing tumour.
03 Scale-matched retraining. Rebalanced the sampler toward small lesions. Overfit the tercile it was tuned on.
04 Centre reweighting. Weight each sliding window toward its own centre instead of averaging flat. Touched no weights at all. Worked.

Net effect, no retraining

+0.145 Dice

Small enhancing tumour goes from 0 to 7 of 21 patients. The simplest thing on the list beat every elaborate one.
