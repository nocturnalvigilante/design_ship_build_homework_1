Matter under examination

The model detects the lesion and cannot locate it

Asked whether a tumour is present, the model answers correctly. Asked where it is, it returns a region many times too large and centred elsewhere, and does so worse the smaller the lesion becomes. Four findings follow. Each is a measurement, not an opinion.

Sustained9 of 9 replicated

01

The collapse is monotonic in lesion size

Held-out patients split into terciles by lesion volume. Dice falls from large to small in all three regions and never recovers.

RegionLargeMediumSmallRatio
Whole tumour0.2810.1370.0574.9×
Tumour core0.1760.0590.0199.3×
Enhancing tumour0.1490.0370.01014.9×

Detection is unaffected throughout, which is what makes any single aggregate number misleading here.

Verdict

14.9×

Worst-case degradation, enhancing tumour, large tercile to small.

02

The lesions are learnable; the conditioning is not

A plain supervised U-Net, handed the same small lesions with no language involved at all, degrades by a factor of 1.2 against 14.9 for the text-conditioned model.

ModelLanguageDegradation
Supervised U-Netnone1.2×
Text-conditionedPubMedBERT14.9×

The data is not the problem.

Verdict

Not the data

Small lesions are findable by a model that is not conditioned on text.

03

The language contributes nothing measurable

PubMedBERT was replaced with random vectors carrying no medical knowledge and no semantics whatsoever. Performance barely moved.

The query functions as an opaque class identifier that happens to be spelled in English. No better prompt and no better encoder was ever going to repair this.

Verdict

Δ ≈ 0.000

Change in Dice when the text encoder is replaced by noise.

04

The cheapest available repair beat every elaborate one

Four interventions were entered on the record. Three were withdrawn after a control caught the error.

  • Size-conditioned prompting — helped small lesions, broke large ones
  • Multi-scale ensembling — gained on tumour core, lost it on enhancing
  • Scale-matched retraining — overfit the tercile it was tuned on
  • Centre-weighted window merging — sustained

The volume is traversed in overlapping sliding windows merged by flat average, which smears a small lesion across everything near a window edge. Weighting each window toward its own centre repairs it and modifies no weights.

Verdict

+0.145

Dice, no retraining. Small enhancing tumour recovered in 7 of 21 patients where the model previously found none.

Finding of the board

A shared confound is not a cancelled confound

The dominant failure was not in the model and not in the language. It sat in the inference plumbing, where nobody was looking, because every stage of a pipeline is an instrument until somebody measures it.

171 paired Wilcoxon tests, Benjamini‑Hochberg corrected. 9 of 9 comparisons replicated across three independent splits.
