25 ways to land one finding.

One idea, twenty-five landing pages. The idea is a research project: text-conditioned 3D medical models detect that a lesion exists but fail to localize it once it is small.

The page has one job. A researcher lands, understands the finding in fifteen seconds, and opens the paper. Everything below is an attempt at that, from a radiology workstation to a CVPR submission to an arcade cabinet.

Versions 01 to 13 go as wide as the subject allows, including directions that were always going to fail. 14 to 19 cross-breed what survived. 20 to 25 converge.
