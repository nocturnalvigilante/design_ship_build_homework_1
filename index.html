25 ways to land one finding.

One idea, twenty-five landing pages. The idea is a research project: text-conditioned 3D medical models detect that a lesion exists but fail to localize it once it is small.

The page has one job. A researcher lands, understands the finding in fifteen seconds, and opens the paper. Everything below is an attempt at that, from a radiology workstation to a Bollywood poster to a transit map to a nutrition label.

Versions 01 to 19 go as wide as the subject allows, including forms it has no business wearing and directions that were always going to fail. 20 to 25 converge: one idea survives contact with the audience, and the last six combine what worked and refine it until v25 is the one I would ship.
